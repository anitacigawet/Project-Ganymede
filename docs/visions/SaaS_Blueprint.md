# Project Ganymede: 9D-Chess as a Service

## The Core Concept
**Ganymede** is an autonomous, high-tier SaaS platform that provides "Prosthetic Metacognition" to executives, businesses, and strategists. It is a unified Generative UI platform where a user inputs a business scenario, and the system dynamically generates both a rigorous theoretical analysis and an interactive 3D physics model of the strategic landscape.

## The Architecture
* **The PKI / Truth Server (NotebookLM):** Operates as the slow, meticulous, highly authenticated logic engine. It pulls only verified, factual data to ensure the strategic analysis is grounded in reality, not AI hallucinations.
* **The Physics Renderer (Gemini):** Takes the mathematical output from the Truth Server and generates the React-based, interactive 3D widgets (the "Sandbox").
* **The Wrapper (Ganymede):** The seamless front-end that orchestrates the split-screen delivery: the undeniable logic on the left, the interactive gravity well on the right.

## The Growth Strategy: "The Addictive Hook"
The product is sold through an undeniable "Time to Value" (TTV) free sample. 
1. **The Bait:** A business owner inputs their current biggest operational bottleneck or upcoming major decision.
2. **The Audit:** Ganymede slowly and precisely maps the hidden multidimensional traps (SDS) they are currently blind to.
3. **The Lock-in:** The user physically sees the trap visualized in 3D. Because the audit is flawlessly logical and saves them from immediate risk, they become structurally reliant on Ganymede for all future high-leverage decision making.
