## Scenario seeds (untested)

### The "Exorbitant Privilege" Collision (Currency / Geopolitics)

A coordinated, overnight announcement by BRICS+ nations to settle all global energy trades (oil/gas) in a new gold-backed Unit of Account (the "R5"), bypassing the US Dollar completely. The 9D test: model the temporal-dynamics window (D4) of a global currency collapse.

### The "Open-Source Neuralink" Black Swan (Tech / Civil Liberties)

An anonymous group releases an open-source DIY blueprint for a high-bandwidth Brain-Computer Interface that is 100% untraceable by traditional neuro-imaging. The 9D test: pits Individual Intelligence against State Surveillance — what does a society look like where anyone can have super-human compute without the government knowing?

### The "Sovereign Sea-Stead" (Geopolitics)

A trillionaire builds a massive modular floating city in international waters, claims permanent legal immunity, hosts its own BCI-based banking system, launches a satellite constellation for "Sovereign Internet." 9D test: what is the Incomprehensible Move the US Navy makes when it realizes it has no legal jurisdiction to board the city?

### The "Agri-Terror" Funnel (Bio / Supply Chain)

A targeted "Data-Blight" cyberattack on the automation software used by the top 5 global seed and fertilizer companies, causing a permanent un-patchable 40% drop in global grain yields starting in 90 days. 9D test: collision between Biological survival, Social order, and Global Supply Chains.

### The "Digital Suicide" Sequence (Civilizational reset)

A specific mathematical sequence is discovered that, when processed by any semiconductor, causes the storage device (SSD/HDD) to physically self-destruct. Sequence is leaked as an "Autoplay" ad on every major social media platform. 9D test: within 24 hours the world has no data, no banking records, no digital history. Society reverts to ~1950 in a day.

### The "Moral Clarity" Signal (Civilizational reset, alternate)

A massive un-removable signal begins emitting from the center of the Arctic. It doesn't damage anything, but anyone who hears it (which is everyone, via the atmosphere) feels Perfect Moral Clarity. No one can lie, no one can act against their own conscience. 9D test: how does a world built on Strategic Ambiguity survive a population that *literally cannot be dishonest*?

### The "Coffee Blight"

A new rapid-spreading airborne fungus makes all coffee plants globally infertile within 24 months. No known cure. 9D test: a simple biological event creating a massive Gravity Well in global commodity markets, labor, and social culture.

### The "Plastic Cliff"

A global treaty permanently bans the manufacture and use of all single-use plastics (food packaging, medical disposables) effective in 30 days. 9D test: a simple legislative move forcing total 9D re-imagining of every supply chain on Earth.

### The "Battery Freeze"

A critical raw material in all lithium-ion batteries (specific high-grade separator film) is permanently contaminated at the source, halting all new battery production for 6 months. 9D test: simple industrial failure that freezes the entire Green Energy / Electronics transition instantly.

## Utility concepts (not committed to)

These were the four "Out-of-the-Box" utilities the Engine itself proposed when asked what the framework could be used for beyond crisis simulation.

### 1. "Genius" Reverse-Engineering (the Strategic Mirror)

Feed Oracles everything a specific high-fidelity mind (Steve Jobs, Satoshi Nakamoto, a specific historical strategist) ever wrote or built. Engine extracts their *Operational Physics* — how they observed the world across 9 dimensions. Creates a queryable Strategic Mirror of that person. *"How would Satoshi Nakamoto solve the current BCI leak?"*

### 2. Institutional Blind-Spot Auditing (Self-Audit)

Feed Oracles every internal document, email, slack of an organization. Engine maps the *Dimensional Gaps*. Identifies where the company is rigid and where it is nuanced. Identifies the SDS the company is sleepwalking into because its CEO/Board has a blind spot in D5 (Mitigation) or D8 (Relational). This concept was later formalized in [`../visions/Alternative_Utility_Blueprints.md`](../visions/Alternative_Utility_Blueprints.md).

### 3. Adversarial Diplomacy (Auto-Mediator)

Two separate Oracle Swarms, one fed each side of a conflict. Engine doesn't pick a winner — it finds the *Overlapping Manifold*, the small 9D space where both sides' nuance can coexist without colliding. A *Mathematical Peacemaker*. Also formalized in `Alternative_Utility_Blueprints.md`. The user's caveat: *"At the same time if we've already seen that it missed out on human context already, I would think it would do the same within that context of being an AI Mediator."* — i.e. the rigidity bias is a real problem for any consequential mediation use, until the Mirror Validation pathway is solid.

### 4. Legacy Synthesis (Historical Archaeology)

Feed Oracles every scrap of data from a complex historical mystery (the Bronze Age Collapse, the 1929 Crash). Engine finds the *Incomprehensible Move* that historians missed. Uses Nuance Prime to identify the social friction that *actually* caused the event, rather than the official story.

## Methodology riffs (preserved for future incorporation)

### Recursive Dialogue Protocol (Oracle-side)

User observation: NotebookLM Oracles tend to volunteer open-ended follow-up questions at the end of their Truth Packets ("Would you like me to elaborate on…"). Idea: route these questions back to the 9D Engine as a "*Question from the [subject] Oracle*" — let the Engine decide whether to drill deeper into the existing Oracle's domain. This converts the Oracle from a one-shot search bot into an active research collaborator.

The user's framing: *"if it wants to query the existing notebook for more information, that's fine and that would be perfect. That would actually be encouraged. What I would be worried about is if it wants to continuously make new notebooks with new subjects."* — i.e. the recursion should always stay within the existing swarm; new oracles always require manual approval.

This is logged in [`../learnings/Iterative_Operational_Learnings.md`](../learnings/Iterative_Operational_Learnings.md) as Insight #4 / Recursive Dialogue Protocol. It is in the Universal Logic Loop as Phase 4. It has not been implemented end-to-end yet.

### Cross-Notebook Querying (Audit query)

User: *"How could we get the notebook to query another notebook? Is that possible or something like that?"* — proposed mechanic: instead of asking an Oracle for facts, ask it for *omissions*. *"You have 10,000 pages of corporate history. Analyze your own corpus through the 9D map. Identify which dimensions are silent. If you have zero data on D8 (Relational Trust), report that as a Dimensional Gap."* This is the engine of the Blind-Spot Auditor utility.

### Engine-modeling-itself-as-parameter

User's late-session refinement of Mirror Validation: have the Engine run its own self-audit ("Map your own existence across the 9 dimensions. What is your D7 weight? Where is your D2 blind spot? What is your internal Friction?"), save the result as a permanent Mirror Profile, and feed it back into every future synthesis as a known bias parameter. The Engine then says things like *"Mathematically the machines win, but I recognize that I am a Pure Logic Actor (per my Mirror Profile) and my own bias is over-indexing on technical speed; therefore the real-world resolution is likely more nuanced."*

This is preserved in the [Mirror Validation pathway](../experiments/pathways/mirror_validation.md) as a sibling approach to the contrast-notebook recursion. The user's preferred mechanism is the contrast-notebook approach; the self-modeling approach is the simpler variant if the contrast-notebook setup is too much overhead for a given run.
