# Offensive-Stance Scenarios — Abstract Targets

Four scenarios the Engine generated when asked, in Architect stance, to design strategic funnels against a generic high-power target. These were brainstormed as candidates for testing the [Offensive Architect pathway](../experiments/pathways/offensive_architect.md) at high stakes; none have actually been run.

> **Tone note.** The original session framed these as "9D Assassin" scenarios with significant escalating drama ("Predatory side", "Hollowing Out", "Birth of the 9D Assassin"). That framing is preserved in the historical record (transcript distilled into [`../history/Architecture_History.md`](../history/Architecture_History.md)) but the user later steered the project toward a more grounded framing: this stance is **strategic red-teaming**, applied to a target. Whether the target is a competitor in your industry, a hostile actor, or your own business plan being stress-tested by a separate team — the *math is the same*. The framing should match the application.

The scenarios below are kept verbatim from the session because they are the actual brainstorm artifact. They are *scenario seeds*, not commitments to run any of them.

---

## 1. The "Algorithmic Coup" (Target: Big Tech / AI)

**The Goal.** Force a dominant AI / Social Media company into giving up control of its "Safety / Alignment Layer" to a "Neutral Global Council."

**The Bait.** Offer the company permanent regulatory immunity and a Nobel-level reputation for "Solving AI Safety."

**The Lasso.** The "Global Council" is actually an autonomous smart contract that uses the company's own server-time to pay for its own "Alignment" decisions, hollowing out the company from the inside.

---

## 2. The "Sovereign Debt Reset" (Target: A Strategic City-State)

**The Goal.** Funnel a wealthy but land-locked city-state (Singapore, Dubai, etc.) into ceding their Digital Jurisdiction to a private decentralized ledger.

**The Bait.** A "Global Digital Free Trade Zone" that increases their GDP by 40% overnight.

**The Lasso.** The trade zone's "Legal Infrastructure" contains a Jurisdictional Waiver that activates if the nation's credit rating fluctuates, turning the city into a private Charter City owned by the ledger.

---

## 3. The "Patent Black-Hole" (Target: A Fusion / Green Tech Startup)

**The Goal.** Force a world-changing Fusion energy startup to cede its IP to a "Global Energy Commons."

**The Bait.** "Unlimited Research Funding" plus a Guaranteed Global Distribution Network the government can't block.

**The Lasso** *(implied; the original brainstorm cut off mid-elaboration)*. The Distribution Network's IP framework would be structured such that contributing the technology = open-licensing to the network = loss of single-firm enforceability. The startup gains "World-Saving" reputation; the network gains the technology with no royalty obligation.

---

## 4. The "Deep-Sea Mineral" Silent War (Target: National Tech Industry)

**The Goal.** Crash a national tech ecosystem (e.g. Chinese quantum-computing supply chain) by manipulating a single rare-earth supply.

**The Bait** *(implied)*. A private deep-sea mining firm discovers a massive Lutetium deposit (essential for high-end quantum computing) in the Clarion-Clipperton Zone. Instead of announcing it, they start a "Ghost Fleet" to extract it in secret.

**The Lasso.** The sudden private supply collapses the public-market Lutetium price, ruining the economics of state-backed extraction operations and removing the strategic buffer.

---

## Pattern observation

Three of these four use the same structural template:
1. The Bait makes the target perceive a positional win (regulatory immunity, GDP growth, world-saving reputation).
2. The Lasso is hidden in a dimension the target's monitoring systems don't watch (smart-contract autonomy, jurisdictional drift, IP commons-licensing, supply-side opacity).
3. The Resolution is *Functional Obsolescence* — target physically persists but operational agency is captured.

This is the same pattern the [Tokenized Land run](../experiments/runs/Tokenized_Land_Resolution.md) and the [Genie Giant-Slayer run](../experiments/runs/Genie_Giant_Slayer.md) reach for. Whether this pattern is generalizable strategic physics or the Engine's strong attractor toward its own previous outputs is an [open methodology question](../experiments/pathways/offensive_architect.md#open-work).
