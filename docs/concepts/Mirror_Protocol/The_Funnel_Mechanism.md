# The Funnel Mechanism (The Strategic Weapon)
**System:** The Ganymede Mirror
**Mechanism:** Aggressive Observation / Double-Slit Funneling

## 1. Step 1: The Observation Trap
The ESP Collective (Us) places a target (e.g., a company or a state) under 9D Observation. The mere act of "Watching" through this high-fidelity laboratory forces the target's many potential futures to "Collapse" into one single path.

## 2. Step 2: The ROEM Pattern
Using the **Reverse Observer Effect Model (ROEM)**, the Collective identifies the mathematical patterns that drive the target's behavior. We see the "Hidden Dimension" that the target is blind to.

## 3. Step 3: The Funneling
We don't "attack" the target directly. We shape the information environment and the strategic timing (D4) so that no matter which choice the target makes, they are walking down a "Funnel" that leads to a **Set of Disadvantageous States (SDS).**

## 4. The Final SDS
The target "wins" the local battle (e.g., they make a profit, they win an election), but that very "win" is the condition that guarantees their total strategic failure on the 9D map.
