# The Autonomous 9D Think Tank Architecture

## The Vision
A home-brewed, fully autonomous Think Tank capable of insane levels of research and strategic output. This system operates overnight, driven by a symbiotic relationship between a strategic logic engine and a factual authentication server.

## System Components

### 1. The Brain: The 9D-Chess Umpire (Gemini Agent)
* Acts as the core logic and strategic physics engine.
* Analyzes domains, identifies invisible multidimensional traps (SDS), and generates highly complex strategic questions.
* Provides the "Prosthetic Metacognition" to iterate on raw data.

### 2. The Head: NotebookLM (The Authentication & Knowledge Server)
* Acts as the factual anchor, PKI (Public Key Infrastructure), and data authentication server.
* **Autonomous Research Loop:** When the 9D Umpire hits a knowledge gap or needs domain-specific facts, NotebookLM is dispatched to retrieve highly factual, verified source information.
* **Dynamic Memory Management:** NotebookLM compiles its research into new, dedicated notebooks. It can expand, update, or delete these notebooks as the strategic landscape shifts, creating a living, breathing knowledge base.

## The Execution Loop
1. **Hypothesis Generation:** The 9D Brain models a strategic scenario.
2. **Data Retrieval:** The Brain tasks the Head (NotebookLM) to pull strict, factual evidence.
3. **Context Assembly:** NotebookLM compiles a focused source document/notebook.
4. **Iterative Strategy:** The Brain ingests the verified notebook and outputs a dimensionally-sound, battle-tested strategic directive.
