## TL;DR

The 9D framework's structural-reasoning **kernel** (DAI/DAP asymmetry,
ROEM's five axioms and five principles, the Convergence Theorem and
Strategic Lasso corollary, the Ω/Ω' perception structure, Incomprehensible
Move) is **genuinely separable** from its aesthetic **scaffolding** (the
9 specifically-named dimensions, Egyptian Set/Horus mythology, Chess/Go
Eastern/Western binary, three-act narrative structure, the 8 Pillars of
Metacognition import). The cleanliness of the partition rests on four
independent empirical anchors:

1. **Mathematical_Formalization_9D_ROEM.md** is ~90% kernel with all
   scaffolding concentrated in ONE section (2.1, the labeled 9-tuple).
   No subsequent theorem, axiom, principle, or corollary references the
   labels — surgical replacement with `Ω = (D₁,...,D_k)` over abstract
   metric subspaces leaves every load-bearing claim intact.
2. **The corpus is internally inconsistent on what the 9 dimensions ARE.**
   `Mathematical_Formalization`, `Metacognition_Mapping`, AND
   `Validation_Adaptations` each enumerate a *different* 9-tuple — three
   incompatible enumerations in the same corpus (e.g., the first lists
   "Mythological" as a single dimension; the third splits it into two
   dimensions, "Egyptian Mythology (Set)" and "Egyptian Mythology
   (Horus)," to make the 9-count work). If the labels were load-bearing,
   internal consistency would be enforced.
3. **The upstream's own `Validation_Adaptations.md` § 3 prescribes the
   partition methodology** ("core principles remain constant, the
   specific manifestation of dimensions will vary significantly across
   fields"). The hypothesis is a literal application of the upstream's
   prescribed methodology.
4. **The metacognition import is already a stripping decision the
   upstream made** — only the 8 Pillars axis was imported from the source
   PDF's 8 × 8 × 8 schema. Further stripping is consistent precedent.

**Recommendation: PROCEED to M2 leaner-corpus side-by-side test.** The
kernel is demonstrably separable; the upstream's methodology endorses
domain-specific adaptation; Risk #2 from the Cleanup Hypothesis (hidden
load-bearing scaffolding) trends LOW. The remaining open question
(Risk #3 — is the issue framework substance or domain fit?) can only be
discriminated empirically. M2's side-by-side run on Powell + Tokenized
Land + Amnesia + Genie Giant-Slayer + LMArena against both the canonical
Engine and a leaner sibling is the right next chunk of work.

**Additional KEEP argument worth naming:** when the Engine operates in
kernel-mode (DAI asymmetry + Strategic Lasso + Incomprehensible Move
applied to a real strategic situation), it surfaces verifiable
real-world precedents — the 1956 AT&T consent decree in
Genie Giant-Slayer, *Collins v. Yellen* in the Powell run, ERC-4337 +
sovereign immunity carve-outs in Tokenized Land. These are real and
findable; the Engine doesn't invent them. When the scaffolding fires
(Set/Horus archetype assignments, three-act narrative mapping), the
output turns into decorative mythology rather than verifiable
mechanism. **Kernel-mode reasoning produces verifiable citations;
scaffolding-mode reasoning produces decorative archetypes.** This is a
sharper criterion than "Powell-class wins" alone — a leaner corpus that
preserves kernel-mode citation-grounded reasoning is the success
criterion; a leaner corpus that loses it is the failure.

## Partition cleanliness assessment

The Cleanup Hypothesis named five risks. M1a's deep read provides direct
evidence on three; two remain forward-looking and require M2 to resolve.

### Risk #1 — "Stripping too aggressively breaks the wins"

**Trends LOW.** The partition is genuinely separable. Direct evidence:

- The math doesn't reference the dimension labels after section 2.1 of
  `Mathematical_Formalization` introduces them. No axiom, principle,
  theorem, or corollary uses the labels operationally.
- The corpus is internally inconsistent on what the 9 dimensions ARE
  (three different 9-tuples in three different files —
  `Mathematical_Formalization`, `Metacognition_Mapping`, and
  `Validation_Adaptations` each enumerate a different list).
  Internal inconsistency is direct evidence the labels aren't
  load-bearing — if they were, consistency would be enforced.
- The upstream's `Validation_Adaptations.md` § 3 explicitly prescribes
  domain-specific dimensional manifestations. The upstream itself
  doesn't treat the labels as load-bearing.

What remains TO PRESERVE in the leaner corpus is structurally
identifiable: Groups 1-6 + 10 (the kernel, strategic primitives, and
validation methodology). What gets DROPPED is also structurally
identifiable: Groups 7-9 (the dimension labels, mythological
scaffolding, metacognition import) + Group 12's rhetorical capstones.

### Risk #2 — "Scaffolding might be load-bearing in ways the outputs don't reveal"

**Trends LOW.** Direct evidence from M1a:

- The math in `Mathematical_Formalization` is fully dimension-label-
  agnostic from section 2.2 onwards. The labels never re-appear after
  introduction. If they were "scratchpad" material the Engine uses
  internally to reach the right insights, we'd expect the math to
  reference them somehow. It doesn't.
- The Powell + Tokenized Land + Genie Giant-Slayer wins are *describable
  in kernel-only language*. Powell's Strategic Lasso = DOJ probe →
  manufactured for-cause requirement → Powell's defense itself
  validates populist narrative; this analysis needs Ω/Ω', DAI
  asymmetry, Strategic Lasso, and "The Game" awareness trigger. It
  needs neither Set nor Horus nor Chess vs. Go.
- The Engine's mythology archetype assignments **inverted across three
  runs of the same scenario** (Anthropic-as-Horus then Anthropic-as-Set
  then back). If the mythology were load-bearing, the assignments
  would be stable. They aren't.

Caveat: this is M1a evidence about the *corpus*. M2 will test whether
the *deployed Engine* still produces Powell-class wins on a leaner
corpus. The corpus evidence is strongly suggestive; the operational
evidence is what M2 establishes.

### Risk #3 — "Domain fit might be the real issue, not framework substance"

**OPEN.** M1a cannot resolve this. The corpus analysis is consistent
with two hypotheses:

- **(A) Framework cleanup needed.** The scaffolding makes the framework
  produce false-positives in unsuitable domains (Amnesia, LMArena
  passive-observer contexts). Stripping the scaffolding would reduce
  false-positives while preserving true-positives.
- **(B) Domain-applicability gate needed.** The framework is fine when
  applied to multi-actor structural-funnel situations (Powell,
  Tokenized) and wrong when applied to others (Amnesia, LMArena). The
  right fix is a gate at the orchestrator level, not framework cleanup.

M1a evidence favors (A) slightly: the corpus's internal inconsistency
and the upstream's own domain-adaptation prescription suggest the
framework was always meant to be domain-tailored. But M1a's evidence
is consistent with both. M2 discriminates: if the leaner corpus
preserves Powell-class wins AND reduces Amnesia/LMArena failures,
that's (A) confirmed. If the leaner corpus produces similar failure
rates on Amnesia/LMArena despite stripping, that's (B) — domain fit
was the issue, not framework substance.

**Meta-level caveat worth naming explicitly:** M1's partition was
synthesized using the same reasoning style the framework produces,
applied to the corpus that grounds that reasoning style. Bicameral
Convergence's audit lens was applied to the corpus that produces
Bicameral Convergence's audit lens. If the partition is wrong in a
way that the framework itself can't catch (because the framework is
what produced the analysis), M1 alone cannot detect it. This is the
deepest reason M2's empirical test is necessary — we cannot reason
our way out of the recursion; we have to run the experiment and
watch what happens to the validated wins.

### Risk #4 — "Notebooks are stateful; re-uploading the corpus is a substantial lift"

**OPERATIONAL, not M1a-discoverable.** Carries forward to M2's
implementation planning. The leaner corpus needs a sibling NotebookLM
notebook with re-uploaded curated source material. Estimated effort:
2-3 hours of source-curation + ~13 individual file uploads via the
`upload_url` / `upload_file` HTTP endpoints (Phase 2 of milestone 30's
notebooklm sub-package refactor surfaced these). Plus persona apply
(the existing canonical Engine persona is small and can be reused
verbatim). Not blocking; just plan-it-in.

### Risk #5 — "The Mirror Auditor's job might change"

**OPEN, but tractable.** If framework cleanup reduces Dimensional
Greed / Pattern-Matching failure-mode firings on Stroke 1, the
Auditor's Stroke 2 has less to catch. Two failure modes to watch for
in M2:

- The Auditor manufactures findings on already-clean input (drift
  from "audit" toward "criticize for its own sake"). Mitigation: the
  Auditor's persona text already includes "if no faults are found,
  say so" — the discipline exists, just needs validation under the
  new substrate.
- The Auditor's role narrows from systematic-failure-catching to
  edge-case-catching. This is a *good* outcome (the architecture
  shifts toward higher-value catches). Worth confirming
  observationally during M2.

## Recommendation: PROCEED to M2

The partition is genuinely separable. The upstream's own validation
methodology prescribes domain-specific adaptation. Risk #1 and Risk #2
both trend low. The remaining open question (Risk #3 — framework
substance vs. domain fit) **can only be discriminated empirically**;
M1a's continued analysis cannot resolve it.

M2's deliverable: a side-by-side test of the canonical Engine against
a leaner sibling on five validated-or-pending scenarios.
